## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Paceora is a daily English study app built for Turkish speakers. Each day you
get a short, focused lesson that fits the time you have; keep a streak, earn XP
and watch your progress grow week by week. The app interface is in Turkish.

**A plan made for you**
• Find your starting level from A2 to C1 with a short placement test.
• A new weekly plan based on your level and daily time (5–60 min).
• One grammar topic and one vocabulary theme every day.

**A lesson every day**
• Vocabulary, grammar explanations, exercises and translation questions.
• Phrases you get wrong go into your mistake notebook and come back in later
  lessons.
• No internet or AI service unavailable? Keep studying with built-in lessons.

**Words that stick**
• Spaced-repetition flashcards with audio.
• Story of the day, matching game, dictation and speaking practice.
• Role-play chats in everyday scenarios.

**Pace keeps you going**
• Pace the cheetah studies with you and celebrates when you finish.
• Keep your streak, buy streak freezes with XP, hit 7, 30 and 100-day milestones.
• Spend XP on new outfits for Pace and pick your accent color.
• Light and dark themes, reduced-motion support, large text support.

**Your privacy**
• No account, no sign-up. Your progress stays on your phone.
• Your name is never sent to the AI service.
• Back up your progress to a single file and restore it on a new phone.

Paceora uses Google Gemini to generate content. You can also use your own free
Gemini API key. See the in-app privacy section and the privacy policy for
details.
